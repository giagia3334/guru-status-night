Guru Status, New Boulevard, Batumi

Home, by way of the lobby.

A finished tower by Guru Holding, with a residents' lobby in glass and stone, a backlit reception, KONE lifts and a heated pool in the courtyard.

Walk through Then see the price

The ground floor is glass, stone and warm wood.

Residents come in through a lobby of their own, kept separate from hotel guests in the building. It was finished in August 2025.

Glass runs floor to ceiling. Light runs through the slatted wood above. The floor is stone-look tile, and the sofas are deep enough to wait in.

A reception lit from behind.

The Guru wall glows between two walls of moss. Concierge, security and technical service are based in the building, so someone is there when you arrive.

Four lifts by KONE.

Passenger and freight, installed and tested in October 2024. The lift halls have marble-look portals and light set into the seams of the wood.

Upstairs, a finished studio you can walk into.

The show studio is furnished, so you can see how a compact apartment lives: the bed by the window, a kitchen bar, a terrazzo shower room. Every apartment has a balcony and floor-to-ceiling windows.

Apartments are sold in frame condition, ready to finish your way, or in one of two turnkey designs by a Batumi design studio.

A heated pool in the courtyard.

The water is heated, so it stays comfortable into the cooler months. Around it sits a 3,000 m² courtyard park with a children's playground, inside a gated area.

Finished, and standing in the sun.
